# RUTHLESS emblem

The brand uses a tall heraldic composition, a central robed figure, two wings, two smaller bowed guardians, an architectural frame, a capital R and the RUTHLESS wordmark.

The palette combines obsidian, charcoal, silver and ivory. Clear silhouettes, restrained ornament and generous margins preserve legibility in the command-center interface.

Earlier variants are retained as `dist/assets/ruthless-divine-sovereign-v1.png` and `ruthless-divine-sovereign-v2.png`. The selected emblem is `ruthless-divine-sovereign-v3.png`; see [current visual specification](BRAND-REVISION.md).
